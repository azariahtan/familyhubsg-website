What is FamilyHub SG

A shared record for everything your family owns and owes

Most households keep this scattered — a folder here, a banking app there, one parent holding the rest in memory. FamilyHub SG replaces that with a single record every member can check, add to, and trust is current.

Household

Every record belongs to a person, not a folder

Add each family member once. Every property, loan, policy and account gets tagged to whoever owns it, so the full picture holds together instead of living in separate accounts nobody else can see.

  • One household, every member's records in view
  • Viewer and editor roles — you decide who can change what
  • Nothing tied to just one person's memory
Projections

See where you're heading, not just where you are

A year-by-year projection of your household's net worth and cashflow, built from the numbers you've actually entered — not a generic retirement calculator guessing at averages.

  • Net worth and cashflow projected forward, year by year
  • Accounts for loan paydown and CPF contributions
  • Click any year to see exactly what's behind it
Reminders

Nothing quietly expires

Insurance renewals, loan repricing dates, policy reviews — flagged before they become a problem, across every member's records at once. One list, not six things to remember separately.

  • Everything due soon, household-wide, in one list
  • Works across property, loans, insurance and investments
Inventory

See it, don't just describe it

Photograph what's in your home instead of trying to describe it. A photo of the actual drawer or shelf tells a family member exactly what you mean — no guessing which cabinet, which folder, which box.

  • Attach a photo to anything in your household's inventory

Our story

Why this exists

This app is built from a personal pain point: too many investments and too much money movement across my parents and I to track from memory. For years, a single spreadsheet held everything — our investments, our home inventory, even health matters — because it was the only place flexible enough to hold a family's worth of scattered information. But a spreadsheet doesn't alert you when a loan is about to reprice, or a fixed deposit is about to mature. FamilyHub SG is the tool I wished I had — one place for everything a family owns, owes, and needs to act on.

Security

Built to be trusted with this

This is your family's financial data. It's treated that way.

  • Row-level access control — every record scoped to your household, enforced at the database
  • Daily backups, automatic
  • Invite members as editors or viewers — you decide who can change records, not just see them
  • Full export to Word or Excel, anytime — your data is never locked in
