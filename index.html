Our story

Why this exists

This app is built from a personal pain point: too many investments and too much money movement across my parents and I to track from memory. For years, a single spreadsheet held everything — our investments, our home inventory, even health matters — because it was the only place flexible enough to hold a family's worth of scattered information. But a spreadsheet doesn't alert you when a loan is about to reprice, or a fixed deposit is about to mature. FamilyHub SG is the tool I wished I had — one place for everything a family owns, owes, and needs to act on.

Security

Built to be trusted with this

This is your family's financial data. It's treated that way.

  • Row-level access control — every record scoped to your household, enforced at the database
  • Automatic nightly backup of your records
  • Invite members as editors or viewers — you decide who can change records, not just see them
  • Full export anytime — Excel, or a complete ZIP with all your photos and documents — your data is never locked in
  • Deleted items and documents stay in a Recycle Bin for 30 days, then are permanently deleted. We recommend keeping an extra copy of important files somewhere else too

Common questions

Where does my data live?

In Singapore. The main database and your uploaded files are hosted in Singapore with Supabase, and stored in encrypted form. A backup copy is kept for 30 days with Cloudflare, so anything deleted by accident can be recovered.

Do you sell or share my data?

No. Your data is never sold and never shared with advertisers. It's used for two things only: keeping your information safe and backed up, and finding and fixing problems so the app can improve. The companies that run and monitor the app (Supabase, Cloudflare, and the analytics and error-reporting tools described in the privacy policy) handle limited data on our behalf. Nobody else gets it.

What do you do with my data?

Nothing beyond storing it, backing it up and keeping it secure. Everything is typed in by you and nothing is connected to a bank or financial institution, so I can't tell whether it's accurate, and I have no interest in finding out. You can even use "Me", "Mom" and "Dad" instead of full names. Because the app is run by the founder and one IT developer, stored data can technically be accessed to keep it working. The privacy policy explains this.

Should I enter full bank account or card numbers?

I'd rather you didn't. As good practice, please don't enter full bank account numbers, credit card numbers or passwords. The name of the bank, or the last 4 digits, is plenty.

What happens if I delete my account?

Your data is removed from the app straight away, and the last backup copies expire after 30 days, so it's fully gone after 30 days. Backups are what let me restore things that were deleted by accident.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes, any time, from Settings. You can download an Excel workbook of everything, or a full .zip that includes your photos and documents. There's also a Word summary of your assets and liabilities, and your home inventory can be downloaded as a CSV file.
