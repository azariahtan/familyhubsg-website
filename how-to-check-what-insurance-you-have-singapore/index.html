How to check what insurance policies you have in Singapore

Last checked 26 November 2026 · 3 minute read

Short answer: use SGFinDex for your own life and health policies. Use CPF's Healthcare dashboard for MediShield Life or an Integrated Shield Plan. For anything else, and for a parent who has died, ask the insurers directly.

Insurer lists: LIA members (life) and the GIA directory (general: motor, home, travel).

Use SGFinDex in 4 steps

  1. Open the app or website of a participating bank or insurer and log in.
  2. Choose SGFinDex and log in with Singpass.
  3. Pick your insurers and log in to each one to give consent. AIA Singapore, Income, Prudential Singapore and Singlife accept Singpass here. The others need their own login.
  4. Go back and retrieve your data.

The 7 insurers are AIA Singapore, Great Eastern, HSBC Life, Income, Manulife Singapore, Prudential Singapore and Singlife. MAS says SGFinDex is currently free. It only returns policies where you are the policyholder or a joint policyholder. A policy your parent owns on your life will not appear.

Data is month-end. HSBC Life updates daily. The others update by the 7th day after month-end, so recent changes may not show yet.

If a parent or relative has died

Older guides send you to LIA's register of unclaimed insurance proceeds. LIA's page now says that register has been permanently removed. LIA says to contact the insurance companies directly.

StepWhat to do
1. List likely insurersCheck their papers, emails, bank statements and will, and ask their agent. This step is our suggestion, not an official one.
2. Contact each insurerUse the LIA and GIA lists above. Each insurer has its own process. LifeSG says to expect to be asked for a death certificate and proof of your relationship.
3. Check CPFIf they were under the Dependants' Protection Scheme, CPF tells Great Eastern Life, which sends claim details to the nominee or the member's address. MediShield Life and Integrated Shield Plan cover ends automatically.
4. Ask about the claimWith no valid nomination, LIA says a Grant of Probate or Letters of Administration may be needed before a payout. Ask a lawyer.

Common questions

Is SGFinDex safe?

MAS says your data is shared only with your consent and is encrypted. SGFinDex itself cannot read or store it.

Why is one of my policies missing?

You may not be the policyholder, the policy may be inactive, the insurer may not take part, or it may not be a life or health policy. Ask the insurer.

Can I use SGFinDex for a parent's policies?

The official pages describe it as a way to get your own information with your own Singpass. For a family member's policies, LIA says to contact the insurers directly.

How do I check my insurance in Singpass?

Singpass is only the login. You open SGFinDex from a participating bank or insurer's app, log in with Singpass, and your policies show there.
